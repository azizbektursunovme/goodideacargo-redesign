Header (doim ko'rinadi): plastinka-logotip, Why Us, Services, Fleet, Drive with Us, Contact, telefon, "Get a quote".
Telefonda: logotip, "Get a quote", menyu tugmasi (320–359px'da faqat logotip va menyu; "Get a quote" menyu ichida).
Footer: logotip, sahifalar, kontakt (telefon, email, manzil xaritaga, ish vaqti PT), © joriy yil, Privacy, Terms.

## `/`
1. Hero: I-90 surati, gantry'dagi yashil belgi: I-5 / I-90 belgilari, "From Renton, Washington", "Freight to all 48 states", "Get a quote", telefon.
2. Qisqa forma: pickup, delivery, sana, xizmat → `/quote/` (shaxsiy ma'lumotsiz).
3. "Three ways to ship with us": 3 xizmat, har birida o'z havolasi.
4. "From request to pickup": 4 qadam (mile-marker raqamlari).
5. "Who you are booking with": kompaniya ma'lumotlari + 3 sabab (+ sharhlar kelsa).
6. "Drive with us" bloki.
7. Yashil CTA: "Have a load this week?".

## Ichki sahifalar
- Why Us: shipperlar uchun, haydovchilar uchun (6 imtiyoz), kompaniya ma'lumotlari.
- Services: 3 xizmat (surat, ro'yxat, o'z quote tugmasi), quote jarayoni, "plan built around your load" CTA.
- Fleet: har mashinada nima bor, uskunalar ro'yxati (yoki zaxira matn).
- Drive with Us: imtiyozlar, bitta ustunli ariza formasi.
- Get a Quote: 2 qadamli forma + telefon paneli.
- Contact: forma (ism, familiya) + telefon, email, ofis, ish vaqti.
