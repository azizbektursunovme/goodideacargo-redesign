Header (eski saytdagidek, doim ko'rinadi): yangi logotip + "GOOD IDEA CARGO", Why Us, Services, Fleet, Contact, "Drive with Us", "Get a Quote".
Telefonda: logotip va menyu tugmasi; menyu ichida hamma sahifalar va ikkala tugma.
Footer: logotip, sahifalar, kontakt (manzil xaritaga, telefon, email, ish vaqti PT), © joriy yil, Privacy, Terms.

## `/` (eski bosh sahifa tartibi)
1. Hero: haqiqiy surat (AI rasm o'rniga), "Reliable Trucking Services / Across the USA", eski matn, "Call Us Now" va "Get a Quote".
2. To'q blok ("LIVE METRICS" o'rnida): 48 shtat, 24/7 dispatch, 3 xizmat, Renton ofisi + "Who you are booking with" (yuridik nom, manzil, telefon, email, ish vaqti; USDOT/MC kelsa o'zi chiqadi).
3. Services: 3 xizmat kartasi (surat, matn, havola) — "Loading..." yo'q.
4. "What Our Clients Say": faqat haqiqiy sharhlar qo'shilganda chiqadi.
5. "Get in touch": manzil, telefon, email, ish vaqti, "Need a quick quote?" kartasi, xabar formasi.

## Ichki sahifalar (suratli banner + breadcrumb)
- Why Us: kirish + chip'lar, shipperlar uchun 3 sabab, 6 haydovchi imtiyozi, 3 tayanch (to'q blok), "Ready to drive with us?".
- Services: kirish, 3 xizmat (surat, ro'yxat, o'z quote tugmasi), "plan built around your load" + 4 qadam.
- Fleet: kirish + surat, 3 standart, mashinalar ro'yxati (yoki zaxira matn), CTA.
- Drive with Us: kirish + 4 imtiyoz, bitta ustunli ariza formasi.
- Get a Quote: 2 bo'limli forma + yon kartalar (telefonda forma birinchi).
- Contact: "Get in touch" bloki.
