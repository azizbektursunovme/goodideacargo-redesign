Effective November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Good Idea Cargo LLC ("Good Idea Cargo", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares personal information when you visit goodideacargo.com, request a quote, apply to drive, contact us, or respond to our ads on Facebook and Instagram. If you have questions, contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Quote requests: your name, email, phone number and company, the pickup and delivery locations, pickup date, service, freight type, estimated weight and any notes.
  • Driver applications: your name, email, phone number, the role you want, CDL type, years of driving experience and any message. We do not ask for your Social Security number, driver's license number or medical card on this website; if you are hired, we collect those documents separately as the law requires.
  • Contact messages: your name, email, phone number, company and message.
  • Facebook and Instagram lead forms: when you submit one of our lead forms on Facebook or Instagram, Meta Platforms shares with us the answers you give in that form, such as your name, email, phone number and freight details.
  • Calls, texts and emails: the content of your communications with our team.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: our hosting provider records your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of the request, to operate and secure the website.
  • Meta Pixel: if enabled on this website, the Meta Pixel uses cookies and similar technologies to measure visits and form submissions from our Facebook and Instagram ads, and to show our ads to people who have visited the site. Meta receives information such as your IP address, browser, the pages you visit and the actions you take. Meta's use of this information is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

How we use information

  • To respond to your request, prepare and send a freight quote, and follow up by phone, text or email.
  • To plan, dispatch, track and invoice shipments you book with us.
  • To review driver applications and contact applicants.
  • To measure and improve our advertising and website.
  • To meet legal, safety, insurance and regulatory obligations in transportation, and to protect our rights.

Text messages (SMS)

We send text messages only if you opt in, for example by checking the optional box on our quote or driver forms. Messages relate to your request, shipment or application. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving messages and HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or application. We do not sell or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in with third parties for their marketing.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with drivers and carriers who handle your shipment, limited to what the shipment needs;
  • with service providers that work for us, such as website hosting, email, phone and text messaging providers, under obligations to protect it;
  • with Meta Platforms, through the Meta Pixel and lead forms described above;
  • when the law requires it, to respond to legal process, or to protect the safety of people or property;
  • as part of a merger, sale or transfer of our business, subject to this policy.

Cookies and your choices

This website itself does not use advertising cookies unless the Meta Pixel is enabled. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, control ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings, and opt out of interest-based advertising at optout.aboutads.info.

How long we keep information

We keep quote requests, messages and applications as long as needed to respond and follow up, and shipment records for the periods required by tax, insurance and transportation rules. We then delete or anonymize them.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email info@goodideacargo.com or call (206) 704-4444. We will respond within the time the law requires and will not discriminate against you for making a request. Residents of some states have additional rights under state law, and we honor them as they apply to us.

Children

Our website and services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be posted on this page.
